BCR Headless 1.1.0

- Added offline transcription with queued processing, speaker labeling, component preparation, and transcript output in TXT or DOCX
- Added recorder output format selection for WAV/PCM, OGG/Opus, and M4A/AAC
- Added automatic audio normalization before transcription so compressed recordings follow the same stable whisper pipeline
- Added stereo-by-default recording with manual mono fallback override and matching stereo or mono component preparation
- Improved transcriber progress, diagnostics, queue controls, and update-state preservation

Full project changelog: https://github.com/wjdob/BCR-Headless/blob/v1.1.0/CHANGELOG.md
